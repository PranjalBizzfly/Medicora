## 2. Certificates (`source-docs/Profile Summary/Degrees & Certifciate/`)

There is no `docs/` folder; certificates are in the git-ignored `source-docs/`. 10 PDFs = 5 unique certificates (each duplicated, identical checksums):

| Certificate | Sensitive details visible |
|---|---|
| BHMS Degree Certificate (MUHS, 2012) | PRN, barcode/serial, photo, Vice-Chancellor signature, seal |
| BHMS Passing Certificate (Nov/Dec 2010) | PRN, seat no., certificate no., signature |
| Registration Certificate (Maharashtra Council of Homoeopathy, 2012) | Registration/certificate no., photo, Registrar signature, seal, validity date |
| MD Degree Certificate (MUHS, 2016) | PRN, serial, barcode, photo, signature |
| MD Passing Certificate (Summer 2016) | PRN, seat no., certificate no., signature |

**Update (client confirmed certificates are a requirement):** all five are now shown on Education & Qualifications as **masked previews** in a "Certificates" gallery inside the existing qualifications section (no new page or section). Each opens full size in a new tab.

How they are made (no wording, dates, names or seals changed):
1. `scripts/render-certificates.cjs` renders each PDF to PNG in `source-docs/certificate-renders/` (private, git-ignored).
2. `scripts/crop-certificates.cjs` crops the certificate from the scan and turns it upright (private).
3. `scripts/mask-certificates.cjs` blurs sensitive details (pixelate + heavy blur) and writes 1200px WebP files to `public/images/certificates/`.

Blurred on every preview: PRNs, registration / certificate / serial numbers, seat numbers, barcodes and signatures. Left visible: name, qualification, university / council, dates, seals and the passport photos printed on the certificates.
