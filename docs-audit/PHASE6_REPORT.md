| Feature | Where | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Light / dark theme | Navbar icon (desktop/tablet); menu drawer header on small phones (≤520px) | Follows the device setting by default; the visitor's choice is saved on the device. Current page marked; focus trapped; Escape closes. |
